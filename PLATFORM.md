# Platform contract

This product lives on the Ergousha IoT platform. What it can rely on, and
what it must do in return, is the SDK's
[PLATFORM.md](https://github.com/ergousha/iot-device-sdk/blob/main/PLATFORM.md),
at the revision `Cargo.toml` pins (`device-sdk = { ..., rev = "<sha>" }`):
replace `main` in that link with the `rev` to read the version this product
builds against.
